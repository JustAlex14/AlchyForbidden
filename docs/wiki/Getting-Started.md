# Getting Started

## The Rift Eye

Craft a **Rift Eye** from an eye of ender, two echo shards and an amethyst shard. Turn it in your hand and it tells you what it wants next.

- Far from anything, it points to the nearest **ancient city**, the way an eye of ender points to a stronghold.
- Near an altar, it speaks of the altar.
- Near one of your structures, it shows the parts the rift will grow there next, as violet ghost blocks.

## The first altar

Go down to an ancient city. The altar works best there, and the sculk is where the Corruption comes from.

1. Craft the **altar core** from an echo shard, amethyst shards, chiseled deepslate and polished deepslate.
2. Build tier 1 around it: polished deepslate under it and a candle on each corner. The Grimoire's Visualize button and the structure preview show the exact shape.
3. Set the Rift Eye on the altar's center. An altar without the eye sleeps. Lift the eye off first if you need to move the altar.

The awake altar takes what you offer, tells you what it wants, and stores the three essences.

## The first rite

The candles become **offering stands**. Lay one item on each, and when the stands hold a rite the altar knows, use the altar to begin. Your first rite costs nothing and gives a **Cracked Vessel** to carry essence in.

From there:

- Gather essences: see [Essences](Essences).
- Grow the altar: see [The Altar](The-Altar).
- Make the other structures from altar rites. Every one is built the same way: tier 1 by hand, then the rift grows the rest.
