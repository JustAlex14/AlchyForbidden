# Familiars and the Rift Maw

## The Rift Maw

The Rift Maw calls a creature out of the rift to be fought, and bound to you if you win. Tier 1 is a 5 x 5 floor with crying obsidian on the corners. It has four tiers.

1. Make the creature's **lure** at the altar.
2. Give the maw the lure and a blank **familiar ring** of the creature's tier, and pay the essence.
3. The rift opens and the creature comes through. A wall rises around the maw, 8 blocks out (10 and 12 at the higher tiers). You cannot leave.

Fleeing, dying, or taking more than five minutes is a loss: the lure and the essence are gone, and the ring comes back blank. A win binds the creature into the ring.

## The void trials (tier 4)

At tier 4 the maw calls the great ones, and does not fight them here. It pulls you into the rift, onto a disc of stone in the void. Players standing on the maw's own blocks are pulled in with you, and players who follow you in join the fight.

- There is no death in the void. A killing blow casts you out, back where you stood, weak and half blind.
- If the one who started the trial is cast out, the trial is lost for everyone.
- The great one must fall within ten minutes.
- Each boss changes as it bleeds. Altered ones change twice.

A win binds the boss into a tier 4 ring. It rides small at your shoulder until something threatens you, then takes its full size. The rift also pays residue and prisms.

## Familiars

A **familiar ring** is worn on the left hand, up to three.

- Call one out or send it back with the familiar key. Only one answers at a time.
- It follows you, fights what hurts you and what you strike, and comes through a rift when you are far away.
- A familiar that falls cracks its ring for three minutes. The maw mends it sooner, for Sorrow. Calling a bound familiar costs nothing.

### Levels

A familiar grows with what it kills, and with a quarter of what you kill, up to level 20. Treats made at the altar feed it: a Sculk Morsel, an Echo Heart, a Void Delicacy. At levels 5, 10, 15 and 20 it learns a trait and a move and keeps them.

## Altered creatures

Sometimes what comes through is altered: **Rifted**, **Deep-Rifted** or **Void-Rifted**. Altered creatures are stronger, harder to kill, and carry affixes. Night helps the odds, a new moon more, the deep dark most. Rift residue given with the lure helps too.

- An altered creature leaves **rift residue** when slain. Releasing a familiar gives more.
- Residue corrupts a familiar one grade further, but one try in four cracks its ring for half an hour instead.
- A **void prism** buys a filter, to keep only the affixes you want.
