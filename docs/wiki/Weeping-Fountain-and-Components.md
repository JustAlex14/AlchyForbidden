# Weeping Fountain and Components

## The Weeping Fountain

A 5 x 5 floor with a basin wall one block high around it. It weeps things you cannot find in the deep dark, paid in essence.

Choose what it weeps, and it makes them one at a time, paying from its own store first, then from sources near it, then from an altar within 24 blocks.

| Tier | Weeps | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | amethyst, crying obsidian, echo shards | normal |
| 2 | adds ghast tears and pearls | 3 times as fast |
| 3 | adds sculk catalysts and wither skeleton skulls | 30 times as fast |

## Components

The fountain also weeps the components that rites, carvings and spells are made of. Nothing else makes these.

| Fountain tier | Component |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ember of Wrath, Tear of Sorrow, Spore of Corruption |
| 2 | Mourning Pearl |
| 3 | Rift Silk |

At the altar, the others are made from those:

| Altar tier | Component |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bone Ink |
| 2 | Rift Alloy, Echo Ink |
| 3 | Void Ink, Abyssal Core |

Every structure past its first tier asks for some of them.

## Kindling

Some components can be used on a structure to speed it up for a while. The time adds up.

| Component | Used on | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Ember of Wrath | Killing Pit | works half again as hard for a minute |
| Tear of Sorrow | Lament Well | works half again as hard for a minute |
| Spore of Corruption | Echo Resonator | works half again as hard for a minute |
| Mourning Pearl | Weeping Fountain | weeps twice as fast for two minutes |
