# The Altar

## The one rule

Every structure in AlchyForbidden works the same way:

1. You build **tier 1** by hand. It forms.
2. You give the core what the next tier asks for.
3. The rift opens over it and **grows** the next tier: pillars rise out of the floor, the platform climbs, parts fly out of the tear to their places, and the core rises with it.

Most structures have three tiers (the Rift Maw has four). Higher tiers can make more and do more.

## Growth

- **It eats some blocks.** Loose ground, stone and sculk in the way are eaten. Anything else stops the growth, and the core tells you which block and where. Clear it and the growth goes on.
- **It is loud.** Near a sculk shrieker, growth shrieks and something in the city answers. Anywhere else it draws monsters out of the dark. Twelve blocks of wool or carpet within five blocks of the core muffle it.
- **It keeps what it earned.** If a part is broken, the structure keeps its tier. Lay the first tier again and the rift grows the rest back for free. A core you carry away keeps its tier too.

## Rites

The candles of the altar become offering stands: four until the third tier, eight after it.

- Lay one item on each stand. When the stands hold a rite the altar knows, begin it from the altar, then step back.
- Taking anything off a stand breaks the rite, and the essence comes back.
- Each rite asks for an altar tier, and many ask for essence.
- Hoppers can lay the offerings, and then the altar begins by itself whenever they make a rite.

JEI lists every rite with its exact items and costs.

## Storage

The altar keeps the three essences, each in its own measure: 400 of each at tier 1, 1,600 at tier 2 and 6,400 at tier 3.
