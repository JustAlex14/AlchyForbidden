# AlchyForbidden

AlchyForbidden is a dark magic mod for NeoForge (Minecraft 26.1.2), built on [AlchyX](https://github.com/JustAlex14/AlchyX). You carve an altar in the deep dark, set the Rift Eye on it, and feed it griefs: the rift grows every multiblock for you, tier by tier, its parts flying out of a great tear in the air. Then you carve the rift into your own body, bind spells into rings, and call creatures out of the void to fight for you.

## Requirements

- NeoForge 26.1.2
- [AlchyX](https://github.com/JustAlex14/AlchyX) 0.3.0 or newer
- Optional: Patchouli (the Grimoire of the Deep), JEI (rites, carvings, spells, creatures and every structure's page), Jade

## The Grimoire of the Deep

With Patchouli installed, the Grimoire of the Deep is the in-game guide, told as the story of a mage who fell into an ancient city. Its chapters open as you go deeper. JEI has a page for every rite, carving, spell, creature and structure, with exact costs.
