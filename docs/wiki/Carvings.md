# Carvings

## The Inscribing Bier

The Inscribing Bier is where you carve the rift into your own body. Tier 1 is a 5 x 5 floor with the core between two blocks and candles on the corners.

Lie down on it and the rift shows your body part by part: head, chest, arms, back, legs, feet and skin. Each carving asks for items and essence, taken from the bier, your body's reserve, your vessels, and the sources and altars near it. JEI has the exact cost of each.

## Room on the body

- A body part takes one carving at the bier's tier 1, two at tier 2 and three at tier 3.
- Your body carries three carvings at first. Each time you lie down and pay more, it makes room for three more, up to 15.
- A carving can be burned away. It hurts, and nothing comes back.

## Stronger together

Each carving past the first makes every carving a fifth stronger. The carvings show on your skin, with veins where they meet. Some carvings use essence while they work (wings use Corruption in the air), drawn from your reserve first, then your vessels.
