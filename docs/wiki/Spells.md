# Spells

## Spell rings

A **Spell Ring** comes out of an altar rite. Rings of tier 2 and 3 are made from the ring before.

- Rings are worn on the right hand, in the order you want them: two at first, and one more for each tier of the Binding Circle.
- A tier 1 ring has one socket, tier 2 two sockets, tier 3 four. Use a gem on a ring to set it.

| Gem | Effect |
|---|---|
| Swift | cooldown -15% |
| Fury | damage +20% |
| Lasting | duration +25% |
| Thrift | cost -15% |
| Reach | range +25% |

## The Binding Circle

A 5 x 5 floor with the core in the middle and candles on the sides. Lay a spell ring in the circle, choose a spell and pay its price (items and essence, listed in JEI). The ring rises and takes the spell. Each ring holds one spell, of its own tier or lower. Unbinding costs Sorrow and a little health.

## Casting

The ring you have selected answers when you cast. Switch rings with the next and previous spell keys (see [Controls and Configuration](Controls-and-Configuration)). A cast takes its essence from your reserve, then your vessels, and each ring has its own cooldown. Spells grow stronger with your carvings.
