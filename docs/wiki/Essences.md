# Essences

There are three essences, which the Grimoire calls griefs. Almost everything costs some of them.

| Essence | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Wrath** | killing. Every hostile creature you kill pours 5 Wrath for every 10 health it had into the vessels you carry |
| **Sorrow** | the dark. Where the light is 3 or lower, a vessel you carry fills by 1 per second. In the deep dark, by 3 |
| **Corruption** | the sculk. A creature that dies within 8 blocks of a sculk catalyst, with an awake altar within 16, gives that altar 4 Corruption and twice its experience. The catalyst does not bloom |

## Vessels

| Vessel | Holds |
|---|---|
| Cracked Vessel | 100 (of whichever essence fills it first) |
| Bound Vessel | 400 |
| Rift Vessel | 1,600 |

Pour vessels into the altar when you come back. Spells and carvings draw from your vessels.

The **Essence Reserve** carving (chest) lets your body hold up to 1,000 of each essence. What you kill and mourn goes there first, and near an awake altar it pours itself in.

## Sources

Sources are structures that make essence on their own and send it to the nearest awake altar, or along their links.

### Killing Pit (Wrath)

A wall two high around a 3 x 3 hole, on a 5 x 5 floor, with spikes in it.

- Each second, each creature inside pays Wrath by its size, and more the longer it has suffered. A death pays a burst.
- The pit keeps the drops and experience for you to collect. It can be set to keep nothing.
- Tier 2 calls monsters in at night. Tier 3 heals any creature under 30 percent health for 5 Corruption, so the suffering goes on.

### Lament Well (Sorrow)

A low ring around a hole on a 5 x 5 floor, candles on the corners.

- A creature that dies within 8 blocks leaves a ghost for 30 seconds, and each ghost weeps Sorrow every second. 12 and 16 blocks at the higher tiers, with more ghosts at once.
- From tier 2 it also takes things of loss: a named item, a broken tool, a music disc, a totem.

### Echo Resonator (Corruption)

A 5 x 5 floor with a sculk shrieker on each corner. It is made at the altar.

- Feed it ender pearls or eyes of ender. It throws one through a small rift from shrieker to shrieker, and each shrieker it passes screams Corruption into the nearest awake altar.
- A pearl breaks 45 times in 100 at each shrieker, an eye of ender only 15.
- Tier 3 wants a warden killed within 16 blocks of it. The rift takes the body and a crystal tears out its heart. From then on it needs no pearls: all four shriekers draw on the heart every 1.5 seconds.

### Rift Knot

A Rift Knot ties a spawner to the rift. What it spawns folds into one creature, up to 64 deep. A folded creature has the health of one and the loot of all of them. In a Killing Pit it suffers for all of them, and in a Lament Well it leaves a ghost for each.

Use the knot on the ground where they should gather (within 32 blocks of the spawner), then on the spawner.

### Essence Infuser

A 5 x 5 floor with a candle on each corner. It pours one essence into a vessel you leave in it. At tier 3, once your body carries an Essence Reserve, you can bind it to yourself: it sends 40 of each essence per second into you, wherever you are.

## Links: Echo Obelisks and the Attuning Fork

An **Echo Obelisk** carries essence on toward where it is needed. It is two blocks tall on the ground, one block on a wall or a ceiling.

Use the **Attuning Fork** on a source, then on each obelisk in turn, then on whatever should receive.

- Each link reaches 24 blocks.
- An obelisk sends down up to 4 branches and skips any end that is full.
- Two linked obelisks carry both ways. Linking the same pair again removes the link.
- An altar receives, and so do most of the structures.
